## Proposed next steps

> **For this first round, please review the content only.** The design, details and finishing are not final yet, and they come in step 3.

1. **Your team reviews the content** (Darwish & Co.)
   Go through the content across the website and send us your notes, drafts or rough ideas. They do not need to be polished or the right length; we take care of that.

2. **We write and apply the final content** (Growthino)
   We turn your input into final copy and apply it across the website. We fit each text to its place in the design and apply SEO good practice to every page.

3. **Design and user experience polish** (Growthino, with your comments)
   Once the content is confirmed, we do a full round of design, layout and interface refinement. At the same time, your team can test the website and send any design comments.

4. **Review together and confirm** (together)
   We discuss your comments, agree on the best approach for each one, apply the changes and confirm the final website with you.

5. **Start the articles** (Growthino, with your review)
   After everything is confirmed, we start writing the Insights articles based on the final positioning.
