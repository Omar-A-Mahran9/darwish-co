## 4. Claims policy

This is the part the audit weighted most heavily, so it is stated as a rule rather than a list.

1. **No claim without a source.** Removed in Entry 1: "Rated 4.9/5 by 200+ clients",
   "Trusted by top-tier companies across MENA", "MENA's leading", "MENA's first",
   "Deeply rooted in MENA culture and regulations".
2. **No superlatives about partners either.** A partner's own published figures may be
   repeated only with attribution to that partner.
3. **No page that promises content it does not have.** Three blog cards for unwritten
   articles were removed.
4. **No contradictory figures.** One counter per fact, hidden when the value is zero.
5. **Partner-delivered work is labelled as partner-delivered.** Confirmed 16 Sep 2026:
   Integrity & Anti-Corruption carries "Delivered with VisionEthics"; Market Research
   carries "Delivered with In Light Consumer Insights". This prevents a partner's
   capability being attributed to Darwish & Co. as its own.
6. **Where a question crosses into legal advice, say so.** The site positions the firm as
   consultants working alongside legal advisers, not as a substitute for them.

## 6. Service structure

Confirmed 16 Sep 2026. Order reflects strategy as the primary offer, matching the homepage.

**Delivered by Darwish & Co.**

1. Strategy & Performance Management — `/services/strategy-performance`
2. Governance, Risk & Compliance — `/services/governance-risk-compliance`
3. Human Resources Consulting — `/services/human-resources`
4. Business Development — `/services/business-development`
5. Digital Marketing — `/services/digital-marketing`

**Delivered with partners**

6. Integrity & Anti-Corruption — `/services/integrity-anti-corruption` — with VisionEthics
7. Market Research & Consumer Insights — `/services/market-research` — with In Light Consumer Insights

Each service has its own page so it can rank and be cited on its own terms, and each
answers: what it is, who it is for, what you get, how an engagement runs, and the
questions buyers actually ask.
