From research findings to a usable executive view

A research engagement should clarify what the evidence means for the decision. The final scope and outputs are agreed around the question, the available evidence, and the client's requirements.

Proposed structure for an executive brief

The decision
The question the research is intended to inform.
The evidence
Relevant market, sector, competitor, or feasibility findings.
The implications
Opportunities, risks, and alternatives for consideration.
The recommendation
A decision-focused synthesis of the findings.

FAQs

Can the research focus on one decision?

Yes. A focused market or competitor brief can be structured around a specific market, investment, entry, or strategic question.

What outputs are available?

Typical outputs are a market study, feasibility assessment, competitive landscape, or executive decision brief, depending on the assignment.

Does every assignment include consumer research?

No. Research methods and specialist involvement depend on the question and agreed scope. Consumer insight can be added through the relevant partner where required.
