FAQs

Do I need to know which service I require before contacting you?

No. We begin by understanding your business challenge and priorities to identify the appropriate advisory scope.

What does an engagement include?

Each proposal defines the advisory sessions, reviews, deliverables and follow-up activities. Any additional work is specified separately.

Do you support implementation or only provide recommendations?

Darwish & Co. provides diagnosis, strategy, advisory oversight and performance review. Specialist implementation is delivered by the entities identified in the project scope.

How are consulting results measured?

We agree success measures linked to the project objective, such as performance improvement, cost reduction or operational efficiency, and review progress against the engagement plan.
