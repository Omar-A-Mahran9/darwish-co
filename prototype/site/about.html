About Darwish & Co.

Better decisions. Stronger institutions.

We are an independent management consulting firm based in Egypt. We help organizations connect strategic priorities with stronger governance, practical execution, and measurable performance.

Our strengths

  • Senior advisory

    Direct access to experienced leadership and specialist expertise.

  • Evidence before recommendation

    Research and structured assessment before solutions are proposed.

  • Practical implementation

    Clear ownership, executable roadmaps, and support for the next step.

Independent advice. Connected thinking.

Darwish & Co. helps government institutions, private-sector enterprises, and development stakeholders navigate complex business challenges through strategic advisory, evidence-based analysis, and practical implementation support.

We treat strategy, governance, people, operations, and growth as connected management issues. Our recommendations reflect how decisions interact across the organization and the realities in which they must be implemented.

We diagnose the business challenge first, then bring in specialist capabilities where the engagement requires them.

Who we advise

SME and growth-stage leaders
Organizations seeking growth clarity, governance discipline, stronger operations, or a structured path to market expansion.
Corporate executives and functional leaders
Decision-makers focused on performance improvement, organizational effectiveness, governance, or strategic execution.
Government and development stakeholders
Institutions requiring locally grounded advisory, structured analysis, and institutional-development support.

Purpose

Help organizations make better decisions, build stronger institutions, and achieve sustainable growth.

Mission

Provide evidence-based strategic advisory and practical consulting support that strengthen governance, improve institutional performance, and translate business priorities into measurable results.

Vision

From understanding the challenge to measurable improvement.

Each assignment is structured around the decision to be improved, the evidence required, the people who own implementation, and the measures that indicate progress.

  1. 01Understand

    Align on strategic priorities, organizational context, and the decision that needs to be made.

  2. 02Diagnose

    Use evidence-based assessment to identify root causes, constraints, and performance gaps.

  3. 03Design

    Develop tailored frameworks, operating models, decision options, and clear roadmaps.

  4. 04Support execution

    Bridge the gap between recommendation and reality through practical implementation support.

  5. 05Measure & improve

    Establish governance to track results, review progress, and refine performance.
