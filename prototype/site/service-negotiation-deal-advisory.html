Strategic Negotiation & Deal Advisory

Enter the negotiation with a clearer position.

Clarify objectives, understand counterpart interests, and structure commercial trade-offs before and during important negotiations.

Discuss your challenge Explore our five stages

When preparation matters most

  1. 01

    Competing interests

    High-stakes commercial discussions involve priorities that do not naturally align.

  2. 02

    Unclear boundaries

    Objectives, red lines, and trade-offs have not been sufficiently prepared.

  3. 03

    Complex structures

    The deal needs stronger strategic positioning and scenario analysis.

A clearly defined mandate

Representation or direct negotiation support is provided only under a clearly defined formal client mandate. It does not imply unrestricted legal representation or signing authority.

Five stages of negotiation support

The engagement is structured around the commercial objectives, the counterpart context, and the authority agreed with the client.

  1. 01

    Position

    Define objectives, priorities, and red lines.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Analyze counterpart interests, leverage, and scenarios.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    Guide discussions and manage trade-offs.

  4. 04

    Structure

    Shape commercial positions and agreement terms.

  5. 05

    Agree

    Support finalization and execution under a defined mandate.

Preparation your team can work with

  • Negotiation Brief

    A clear statement of objectives, priorities, and boundaries.

  • Stakeholder Map

    A structured view of the parties and interests relevant to the negotiation.

  • Deal Scenarios and Trade-Off Analysis

    A basis for comparing options and understanding commercial choices.

  • Negotiation Strategy and Term Positioning

    A considered approach to discussions and agreement terms.

Better preparation. More disciplined decisions.

  • Stronger deal positioning.
  • Better-prepared negotiation teams.
  • More structured agreements and decision discipline.

The purpose is to strengthen the quality of preparation and commercial decision-making. The final agreement depends on the parties, circumstances, and agreed mandate.

FAQs

Can you support preparation without attending the negotiation?

The scope can focus on objectives, counterpart analysis, scenarios, and negotiation strategy. Direct participation is separately defined within the client mandate.

Can you negotiate on our behalf?

Representation or direct negotiation support requires a clearly defined formal client mandate. It does not imply unrestricted legal representation or signing authority.

What are the typical outputs?

Outputs may include a negotiation brief, stakeholder map, deal scenarios and trade-off analysis, and negotiation strategy and term positioning.
