# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing codebase: Vite + React + wouter, server-side prerendered (`scripts/prerender.mjs`), Express server, drizzle ORM. Static content lives in `shared/` (services, SEO).

## Users
Owners and senior managers of family-owned or founder-led SMEs in Egypt and the wider MENA region, who need governance, controls and growth strategy that investors, major clients and regulators expect. Two priority buying situations lead the homepage (see `docs/positioning.md` §3).

## Product Purpose
Website of Darwish & Co., an Egypt-based governance and management consultancy headquartered in New Cairo. It lets an owner recognise their situation, understand what they receive, who delivers it and roughly how long it takes, and book a consultation. It also hosts guides and articles, a partners area, a client login and an admin inbox.

## Positioning
Every claim on the site is traceable to a named person, a real work product or a published source, and each service states who delivers it (Darwish or a named partner). Anything that cannot be traced is an offer under development, not a service. Approved line: "A wise word in the right moment."

## Operating Context
Family ownership, relationships and hierarchy are design constraints, not excuses. The audience is Egypt/MENA; delivery footprint is stated separately from target market (Egypt until wider coverage is confirmed). Contact is by form, email, phone/WhatsApp and a consultation booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Five services on separate pages, in three groups with governance leading; guides library; blog; partners and corporate partners pages; contact form with inbox and email forwarding; client login and admin client access.
- English-only today; the founder's values exist in Arabic and are kept beside their translations in code. Arabic publication is not decided.
- Nothing has been published: work is local edits and local preview only until the user says otherwise (confirmed).
- Facts withheld pending confirmation are listed in `docs/entity-facts.md`; withheld stays withheld.

## Brand Commitments
Public brand spelled exactly "Darwish & Co." Voice (from `docs/positioning.md`, carried from the reviewer): calm, plain, precise, advice from someone who has seen the problem before; lead with the owner's situation, then what changes, then what they get. Never "world-class", "leading", "comprehensive", "tailored solutions", "top-tier", "unlock", "bold", or unsourced ratings and client counts.

## Evidence on Hand
Documented in `docs/` (positioning, entity facts, content plan, change log, where-we-are). Published guides and articles exist. No client ratings, client counts, testimonials or pricing are confirmed; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Traceable over impressive: no claim without a person, work product or source.
2. Lead with the owner's situation, not the firm's service catalogue.
3. Say who delivers, what they receive and roughly how long.
4. Governance is the distinguishing focus; growth strategy is the outcome.
5. Keep the audience claim (MENA SMEs) separate from the delivery claim (Egypt).
