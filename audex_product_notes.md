## Brand / Section Direction
- Product family slide presents **Audex Compass**, **Audex Command**, and **Audex Perfect** as the three products under one Audex family.
- Visual language: dark navy base, white surfaces, cyan/teal, green, orange, and blue accents; clean enterprise product cards; school / executive decision context.

## Audex Compass
Source slides: product intro and product detail slides around pages 8-10 in the presentation.

### Core promise
- **Test Before You Commit**
- Helps leadership understand future possibilities before committing resources, investments, or strategic decisions.

### Key verbs / functions
- Simulate
- Forecast
- Compare
- Evaluate

### Strategic simulation intelligence details
- Simulate future scenarios
- Compare strategic options
- Test operational readiness
- Evaluate financial impact
- Identify risks and opportunities
- Prioritize where attention is needed first
- Supporting line: **Know where to act before deciding how to act.**

### Investor framing
- Before starting: investment feasibility, revenue projections, break-even and payback periods, capacity requirements
- Expanding a school: expansion scenarios, licensing readiness, accreditation readiness, market and inflation sensitivity, conservative / balanced / aggressive / stress scenarios
- Supporting line: **Test before committing capital.**
